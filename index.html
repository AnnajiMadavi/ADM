ABOUT THE PROFESSOR

Teaching economics with a focus on understanding the real world.

I am Dr. Annaji Madavi, an Associate Professor of Economics. My full name is Annaji Dayaram Mandavi. I am currently working as an Associate Professor in Economics Department at Adarsh Education Society’s Arts, Commerce & Science College, Hingoli, Maharashtra. I was born in a very small village and I had a tribal family in Dongaratamashi in Gadchiroli district, a tribal and forest-rich district of Maharashtra. I completed my undergraduate education (B. A.) at Adarsh College Desaiganj (Wadsa), and Post graduation (MA) from Nevjabi Hitkarini College, Brahmaputra. I have cleared the SET Examination from UGC- Pune University, and I have received a Ph.D. from S.R.T.M. University, Nanded. My academic interests centre on teaching, economic analysis, research and helping students connect economic concepts with contemporary social and economic issues.

This website is created as a student-friendly academic hub where learners can find course material, syllabus, recommended books, announcements and useful resources without having to search in multiple places.

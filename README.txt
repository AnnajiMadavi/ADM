# Economics Professor Website

This is a beginner-friendly static academic website.

## Files
- index.html — website content
- style.css — design and responsive layout
- script.js — search, filtering, mobile menu and small interactions
- images/profile.jpg — replace with your photograph
- documents/notes/ — put your notes PDFs here
- documents/syllabus/ — put official syllabus PDFs here
- documents/books/ — put book PDFs/details here

## Quick setup
1. Open `index.html` in a browser to preview the website.
2. Replace the sample text in `index.html` with your own details.
3. Put your photo at `images/profile.jpg`.
4. Put PDFs in the folders above using the exact filenames used by the links, or change the link filenames in `index.html`.
5. For a real contact form, connect the form to an email/form service or your own backend.

## Important placeholders to replace
- College / University name
- Official email
- Department details
- Books and publications
- Notes and syllabus
- Announcements
- Profile photograph

The website is intentionally built without a framework so it is easy to edit and host.
